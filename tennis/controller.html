Enter the code on the big screen.

How to play: hold the phone like a racket handle. Two of you can play each other, or team up against the computer (switch at the top of the screen). Your player runs to the ball by themselves; you just swing, no buttons. Swing when the ring around the ball closes. Swing early to pull it across the court, late to send it the other way; swing harder to hit harder, or gently for a lob. To serve, tilt your phone up (like lifting your arm to toss the ball), then swing as the ball comes down. Tapping SERVE tosses it too.
