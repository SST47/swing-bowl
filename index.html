How to bowl

  1. Use ◀ ▶ to set where you stand.
  2. Press and hold the big button. Swing your arm back, then forward like a real bowl.
  3. Let go of the button as your arm comes forward. Faster swing, faster ball.
  4. The ball goes where you swing: straight back and through for a straight ball, pull left or right to send it that way.
  5. Twist your wrist as you release to put hook on the ball.

Hold the phone firmly! On iPhone, Safari asks for motion access when you tap Connect. Tap Allow. Both devices just need internet: Wi-Fi or cellular, same network or not.
