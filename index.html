How to play

  1. Open a game on a laptop or a TV's web browser: that's the big screen.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code with their phone camera (or opens the link and types the 4-letter code).
  3. Tap Connect, then Allow when the phone asks for motion access.
  4. Swing! Grip the phone firmly. The phone shows what it measured after every swing.

Wi-Fi or cellular both work; the phone and the big screen don't need to be on the same network. No second screen? Try bowling solo on one phone. The host can remove a player from Players / QR on the big screen.
