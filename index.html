How to play

  1. Open a game on the TVAny laptop or smart TV web browser works. That screen becomes the stadium.
  2. Scan the codeEveryone points their phone camera at the code on the TV, then allows motion.
  3. SwingGrip your phone firmly and swing it like the real thing.

No TV handy? Bowl on just your phone.

Phones and the TV don't need the same Wi-Fi, and you can switch games mid-match: every phone follows.
