🎳 Solo bowling

Your phone is both the lane and the ball. Hold the big button, swing the phone like a bowling ball, and let go of the button as your arm comes forward. The ball goes where you swing. Then watch the pins fly.

Grip it tight. Don’t actually let go of the phone!

Want a big screen and friends? Open the Swing Bowl big screen on a laptop or TV and join with each phone.
